# Skills learnt in THM
## Pre-Security:
In the first section of THM, you are introduced to the digital world, but from the perspective of cybersecurity. You’ll learn about the hardware components involved in security, such as HSMs, TPMs and SSDs, amongst others. You’ll also explore cloud architectures such as IaaS, PaaS, SaaS and the new FaaS services.
You’ll also be introduced to command-line interfaces in Windows, though the focus is more on Linux and its advantages over graphical user interfaces.
To complement these sections, they introduce the workings of cryptography using basic examples, the importance of network architecture in upholding one of the most important principles of cybersecurity – the CIA – the different types of networks such as LANs, WANs and VPNs and how they work, an in-depth understanding of the OSI model, the vital role of firewalls as the first line of defence and their positioning; a brief introduction to the different types of communication protocols, such as TCP, SSH, HTTP and their secure versions, as well as messaging protocols; and a visual demonstration of how they work with SNK/ACK in Wireshark.
And to conclude, it reviews the importance of the CIA triad, the ever-present role of cryptography, how operations are carried out from the perspective of an ethical hacker, and the vital role played by the SOC as a line of defence, a corrective measure and a development function, whilst providing a brief overview of tools such as SIEM, SOAR and EDR/XDR.
